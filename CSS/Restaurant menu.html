> Suda suda Idli

Suda Suda Idli is a popular South Indian breakfast dish made from soft and fluffy steamed rice and urad dal batter. It is served hot with sambar, coconut chutney, and tomato chutney. The idlis are light, delicious, and easy to digest, making them a perfect choice for breakfast or dinner.
